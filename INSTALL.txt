Installing StompBox in Logic Pro
================================

1. Unzip StompBox-AU.zip. You get StompBox.component.

2. Move it into your Components folder:
     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   (In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path above.)

3. The build is not notarized by Apple, so clear the download flag once.
   Open Terminal and run:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/StompBox.component

4. Open Logic Pro. If StompBox doesn't show up, open
   Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

5. On a guitar track: Audio FX slot > Audio Units > Levek11 > StompBox.
   Choose Mono->Stereo on a mono DI track, so each Royal 30 gets its own side.

Loading your cab IR: open the Amp / Cab tab and click "Load IR...".
The IR is copied into the Logic project, so it keeps working even if the
file moves.
